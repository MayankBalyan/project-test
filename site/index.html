How it
works

  1. 01

    Focus to plant

    Start a focus session. When it ends, a seed lands on your island. Longer sessions plant bigger things, from flowers to rare oaks.

  2. 02

    Habits water it

    Every day you check off a habit waters what you planted. Three watered days and a plant is fully grown.

  3. 03

    Streaks grow the world

    Keep showing up and the island changes: a stream at 7 days, birds at 30, a waterfall at 100, seasons at a year.

Streaks

Every day,
in ink

A year-long grid like GitHub’s shows every day you showed up. The darker the square, the more you did.

  • A streak for each habit, and one for you
  • Rain Days save your streak on a missed day, one earned every 7 days
  • Late night? The day ends at 4 AM, not midnight

Focus

Deep work,
quietly

A Pomodoro timer or a stopwatch, with 10, 25, 50 or 90 minutes. Start it on your laptop and it’s the same countdown on your phone.

  • Sessions can fill habits like “read 30 minutes”
  • Optional Stay Focused mode: leave the app and the plant wilts
  • A reminder when your session ends

Your island

A world that
remembers

Your island floats in space and grows from your real effort. Tap any tree to see the session that planted it.

  • Grows bigger with every 10, 50, 100 hours of focus
  • Given-up sessions leave a wilted sprout, never a dead tree
  • Finish your next session to bring it back

Habits

Your habits,
your way

Tick it off, count it, or time it. Every day, some days, a few times a week, or every few days.

  • Done / not done, counts (8 glasses of water) or minutes
  • Up to 3 reminders per habit, skipped once it’s done
  • An evening nudge if your streak is at risk

Questions

Is Istel free?

Yes. Everything in Istel today is free, with no ads.

Do I need an account?

Yes, but it takes a few seconds: enter your email and type in the code we send you. No password. Your account keeps your habits, streaks and island safe and in sync on every device.

Is it on Android and iPhone?

The Android app is coming to Google Play soon. On iPhone, iPad or any computer, use the web app at app.istel.space. In Safari, tap Share → Add to Home Screen to keep it like an app.

Does it work offline?

Yes. Everything you do is saved on the device first. If you’re signed in, changes sync the next time you’re online.

What happens if I miss a day?

Every 7-day streak earns a Rain Day (you can hold 3). A missed day uses one automatically and your streak lives on. If a streak does break, your best is kept and the island never loses what it grew.

How is this different from Forest?

Instead of one tree per session, you grow one island over time. Habits water it, streaks unlock new parts of the world, and giving up only wilts a sprout. It also tracks your habits, not just focus.

How do I delete my data?

In the app: Account → Delete my account, or Settings → Erase all data on this device. Or follow these steps.
