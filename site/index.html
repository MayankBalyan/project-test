Habits & streaks

Daily, weekly or every few days. A year-long grid shows every day you showed up, and Rain Days protect a streak when life happens.

Deep focus

A Pomodoro timer or stopwatch that stays in sync across your devices. Finished sessions can count toward habits like “read 30 minutes”.

Your island

Every focus session plants something. Days with habits water it. Streaks unlock a stream, birds, a waterfall and seasons.

Yours, and quiet

No ads, no tracking, no selling data. Use Istel without an account and everything stays on your device, or sign in to keep your phone and laptop in sync.
