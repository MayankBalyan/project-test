Delete your
account

Istel · by Mayank Balyan

In the app (fastest)

  1. Open Istel on your phone, or app.istel.space on a computer.
  2. Tap the account button (your initial) at the top of the Today screen, or Sign in first.
  3. Scroll to Delete account. Choose whether to also erase the data on this device.
  4. Tap Delete my account, then tap again to confirm.

Your account is deleted straight away.

Without the app

Email support@istel.space from the address you sign in with, with the subject “Delete my Istel account”. We’ll delete the account within 7 days and reply to confirm. We may ask you to confirm the request from that email address, so nobody else can delete your account.

What gets deleted

  • Your account and email address.
  • All synced habits, check-ins, focus sessions, the running timer, your settings and island name.

Nothing is kept on our servers after deletion. Copies in our providers’ routine backups and logs expire within 30 days. Data saved only on your own device stays there unless you choose to erase it (or uninstall the app).

Only want to remove data from one phone? Use Settings → Erase all data on this device. That leaves your account and synced data untouched.
