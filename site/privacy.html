Privacy
policy

Effective 1 October 2026

Istel is a habit tracker, focus timer and island game for Android and the web, made by Mayank Balyan (“we”, “us”). This policy explains what the app stores, why, who helps us process it, and how you can get it or delete it. If anything here is unclear, write to support@istel.space.

In short: Istel needs an account. We store your email and your Istel data so you can sign in and keep it in sync between your devices. No ads, no analytics, no tracking, and we never sell your data.

What we store

You sign in to Istel with your email and a one-time code. Your account lets you use Istel on more than one device. We store:

DataWhy
Your email addressTo sign you in with a one-time code and to recognise your account.
Habits: names, schedules, goals and reminder timesSo your habits appear on all your devices.
Check-ins (which habit, which day, how much)To calculate streaks and the activity grid everywhere.
Focus sessions (date, length, tag, finished or not) and a running timerFor focus stats, your island, and the timer that follows you between devices.
Settings (island name, focus length, day-start hour, notification choices) and your time zoneSo the app behaves the same on every device and days end at the right time for you.
Account dates (when it was created and last signed in)Needed to run the account securely.

We don’t collect your name, contacts, location, photos, device identifiers, advertising IDs or payment details. The app has no ads and no analytics or tracking tools.

A copy of your data is also kept on your device (or in your browser on the web) so Istel works offline. Reminders are scheduled by your device’s own notification system and don’t pass through our servers.

Who processes your data

  • Supabase hosts the database and sign-in for accounts.
  • Resend delivers the sign-in code emails to your address.
  • Vercel hosts this website and the web app, and keeps short-lived technical logs (such as IP addresses) to run and protect the service.

They only process data to provide these services to us. Data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS), and our database only lets each signed-in person read and change their own rows.

How long we keep it

Account data is kept while your account exists. When you delete your account, your account and all synced Istel data are removed from our database immediately. Copies in the providers’ routine backups and logs expire within 30 days.

Your choices and rights

  • Get a copy: Settings → Export JSON or CSV exports everything Istel has on your device.
  • Correct it: edit habits, settings and your island name any time in the app.
  • Delete it: Account → Delete my account, or follow the steps on istel.space/delete-account.
  • Ask us: email support@istel.space for any request about your data. We reply within 30 days.

You can turn notifications off in the app’s Settings or in your device settings at any time.

Children

Istel is not directed at children under 13, and we don’t knowingly collect personal data from them. If you believe a child has created an account, contact us and we’ll delete it.

Changes

If this policy changes, we’ll update the date above, and for important changes we’ll tell you in the app before they apply.
